How it works

Three steps. No friction. No downloads.

Step 1

Create a room in seconds

No account required to join. Generate a code, share it, and you're in. Confer runs entirely in your browser — no installs, no plugins, no waiting.

Step 2

Invite who you need

A simple invite code, offer, and answer is all you need, plus Confer will do all the work for you. Participants join instantly from any device — desktop, tablet, or phone. One link, everywhere.

Step 3

Talk like you're there

HD video and amazing streaming. Screen share, chat, and record — all inside the same clean window. Plus, you don't need internet thanks to WebRTC and wireless LAN.
